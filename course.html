Why the Wheel Strategy?

Most people are terrified of options because they think of them like lottery tickets. And if you buy options, it is a gamble.

The Wheel Strategy flips the script. Instead of paying money and hoping to get lucky, you act like the casino. You sell the options, collecting non-refundable premiums upfront.

It's a conservative, repeatable process that allows you to get paid while waiting to buy stocks you love at a discount.
